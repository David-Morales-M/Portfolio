About me

The person behind the code

I build software that genuinely solves problems, technology that feels natural to use, regardless of who's using it.

My journey has been far from conventional. While most developers build portfolios of side projects, I began my career in academic research, co-authoring 13 peer-reviewed publications on usability, accessibility, and serious games.

Two projects define what I care about most: Ludominga, a serious game platform for people with intellectual disabilities, and PAR, a telerehabilitation tool for respiratory therapy patients, both free, open-access, and built to reach the people who need them most. That's the kind of work I want to keep doing.

I'm detail-oriented by nature and by training. I don't consider something finished until it meets the need it was built for, not just technically, but from the user's perspective.
